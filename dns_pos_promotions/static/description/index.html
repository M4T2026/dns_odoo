Overview

Odoo POS Promotions replaces ad-hoc pricelist tricks with one workspace for creating, scheduling and auditing offers. Pick the stores and products an offer applies to, choose a reward, and validate. The module checks for overlapping offers before anything reaches a till.

Validation runs in a single database transaction. Selectors are resolved into an indexed product and store scope, and conflicts are checked under a PostgreSQL advisory lock. Any overlap of product, store and time moves the offer to Conflict. Version 1 deliberately has no stacking or override.

NOTEEditing a live promotion: changing dates, stores, rewards, selectors or assortment groups returns it to Draft until validated again, so tills never drift out of sync.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you buy.

Does it work with Odoo Community and Enterprise?

Yes. The module is built for Odoo 17 Community and Enterprise.

Can two promotions apply to the same product at once?

No. Version 1 has no stacking or override. If two offers overlap in product, store and time, the later one is flagged as Conflict and listed in the conflict queue.

What happens when I edit a live promotion?

Changing dates, stores, rewards, selectors or assortment groups returns it to Draft until you validate it again.

Do promotions work on offline tills?

Yes. Offline tills check cached offers against their local end time, so an expired offer is never applied.

How are bundle prices split across items?

Only whole sets get the bundle price, allocated in whole cents. With “Any 3 for $5” and 5 items, 3 are priced at $5 total and 2 at the normal price.

Can I restrict who manages promotions per store?

Yes. Assign the Backoffice or Viewer role and set Allowed POS to limit a user to specific tills.
