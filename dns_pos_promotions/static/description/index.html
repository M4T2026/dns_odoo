Open Odoo Promotions from the main menu to launch the standalone workspace, then follow the flow below.

  1. 1. BasicsName, offer type, priority, automatic.
  2. 2. StoresAll stores, groups or individual tills.
  3. 3. ProductsCategory, tag, vendor or barcode selectors.
  4. 4. RewardPercent, fixed, bundle total, free item.
  5. 5. ScheduleStart and end in the promo timezone.
  6. 6. ReviewSave draft or save and validate.
Draft→ validate →ScheduledorActiveorConflict→ finallyEnded

Validation runs in a single database transaction: selectors are resolved into indexed product/store scope and conflicts are checked under a PostgreSQL advisory lock. Any overlap of product, store and time moves the offer to Conflict. Version 1 deliberately has no stacking or override.

Editing a live promotion changing dates, stores, rewards, selectors or assortment groups returns it to Draft until validated again, so tills never drift out of sync.

Offer types

TypeUse it for
Product Percentage DiscountA percentage off a resolved product list.
Product Fixed Amount DiscountA flat amount off each eligible product.
Mix and Match BundleA fixed total for a required quantity; multiple groups must each be met (e.g. two drinks and one snack).
Buy X Get Y FreeA specific free product once the condition is met.
Order Total Percentage DiscountA basket-wide percentage, no product list needed.

Automatic pricing

Calculated from the current base price as items are added. No promotional pricelists.

Whole sets only

“Any 3 for $5” with 5 items shows 3 for $5 plus 2 at the normal price, allocated in whole cents.

Safe & auditable

Promoted lines are protected from manual edits; refunds and reprints keep the original pricing.

Offline tills check cached offers against their local end time, so an expired offer is never applied. If data changes mid-order, the order keeps its snapshot and the new version loads for the next empty order.

Installation

1. Copy dns_pos_promotions into your Odoo 17 addons path
2. Update Apps List and install "Odoo POS Promotions"
3. Settings > Users > assign a Promotion role (set Allowed POS for Backoffice / Viewer)
4. Open Odoo Promotions from the main menu

Depends on point_of_sale, mail, bus.
